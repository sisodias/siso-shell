Demand posture

Source frame is the Action Model system-map industry slug law_firms: taxonomy reference only, not client demand or implementation proof. Law firms are the N1 research pilot.

Automation research

An occupational task inventory (O*NET) identifies legal-assistant document preparation, records organisation and research. A preregistered 2024 study found errors in the legal AI research tools it tested despite retrieval grounding. This supports decomposing tasks and measuring supervised assistance; it does not supply a law-firm-wide automation rate. forecast_percent: null.

Claim ceiling

This is not legal advice, product admission or an industry-wide value forecast. Current software adoption and subscription prices were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed. No claim that a generic repository replaces the whole practice stack.

The offer

The offer

A supervised operating layer over the firm's existing software: intake normalisation and chase, approved-template assembly, citation-linked document review, signing coordination, approved client updates, billing-exception review. Each process carries a metric, a falsifier and a named human authority; the offer is sold on measured human minutes and first-year net cash in one authorised firm, never on a chart.

Pilot design

Within one authorised firm, compare matched routine work under the incumbent workflow and the supervised candidate workflow. Start with synthetic/redacted fixtures; pre-register which documents/actions are in scope before using live data.

  • units processed
  • baseline human minutes
  • assisted human minutes including review and rework
  • critical errors and denied-access cases
  • actual cancelled spend
  • recurring operating cost
  • implementation and rollback cost
